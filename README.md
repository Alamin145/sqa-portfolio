##  Testing Approach

For every project, I follow a structured and risk-based testing approach:

1. **Understand business requirements and workflows.**
2. **Analyze functional and business requirements.**
3. **Identify critical business scenarios and test conditions.**
4. **Prepare test scenarios and test cases.**
5. **Execute functional and integration tests.**
6. **Validate business rules and application behavior.**
7. **Perform database and data consistency validation where required.**
8. **Identify, document, and track defects.**
9. **Retest resolved defects.**
10. **Perform regression testing to ensure existing functionality remains stable.**
11. **Support UAT and client-side validation.**
12. **Perform release verification before production deployment.**
13. **Investigate production issues and validate fixes.**
